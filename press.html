Painting Transformations

The text explores Hayan Abdul Jabbar’s view of the relationship between graphic art and oil painting. He explains that the same subject can be produced through both approaches, with the main differences lying in the methods, materials, and tools of execution, while oil painting allows him to work on a larger scale. He reflects on the influence of his education at the Institute of Fine Arts and the guidance of several leading artists, particularly Rafa Al-Nasiri, whose teaching instilled in him respect for materials, tools, subject matter, and the fundamentals of artistic practice. The text presents his work in both graphic art and oil painting as part of a long-term process of research and artistic production.
